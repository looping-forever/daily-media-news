# Description
A weekly scheduled workflow probes every AI family independently of the digest and exits non-zero with a single actionable line naming the family, its status, and the concrete fix, so degradation is caught in days instead of surfacing as a quietly thinner edition weeks later. One line must be enough: the owner should be able to act without opening logs.

Scope: the scheduled workflow, the per-family probe, and the one-line failure output. Out of scope: publishing, digest generation, and any auto-remediation of keys or model ids.

# Why
The digest is designed to degrade gracefully, which is the right behaviour for readers and the wrong behaviour for the owner, because nothing announces that a rung has been dead for a month. The owner's goal is years of unattended operation, and unattended operation needs a signal that is impossible to miss and cheap to act on.

# Acceptance Criteria
- A weekly schedule, independent of the digest workflow, so a digest bug cannot suppress the canary.
- A failure prints one line: family, HTTP status or error class, and the concrete action to take.
- A healthy run prints one row per family with its status, matching the existing probe rows.
- The canary never runs the digest walk and never publishes.
- A family that is merely slow is not reported as dark; the canary distinguishes a timeout from a refusal or a missing credential.
