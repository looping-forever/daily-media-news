[NEW]C++26 fails its ISO draft ballot over contracts
2026-10-25

Reported: C++26 failed its Draft International Standard ballot, the first C++ draft to do so, according to a post summarising the official SC22 result. The draft got 12 of 20 P-member yes votes (60%, short of two-thirds) and 9 of 22 negative votes (41%, above the 25% cap); the ballot closed on 23 October 2026. It is not yet known how long the delay will be or whether contracts will be removed.
